# v0.6.0 production validation checklist

Before release, validate on one real installation in small steps:

1. Update only the AppDaemon Pool Manager code and configuration; keep forced-stop and freeze safety available.
2. Select `Début de saison • Smart` only when a deliberate full warm-up is acceptable; confirm that reaching the setpoint switches the selector to `Automatique`.
3. In `Automatique`, confirm the predictive diagnostic sensor receives daily forecasts and shows `🏊`/`🔥` markers without starting the PAC unexpectedly.
4. Confirm one planned start: pump starts first, minimum PAC flow is confirmed, then climate switches to Heat/Smart.
5. Confirm one planned stop: climate switches Off first, post-circulation completes, then normal filtration strategy resumes.
6. During a poor-weather period, confirm the PAC does not maintain full setpoint continuously and respects the automatic reserve floor.
7. Confirm `Fin de saison • Smart` allows the larger configured reserve delta.
8. Confirm `Hors Gel`, forced stop and PAC-flow fail-safe still override predictive demand.
9. Observe learned PAC °C/h values over several real heating cycles before tuning the configured baseline.

Do not validate several new behaviors at once on unattended equipment.
