# v0.6.0 — Season-wide predictive heating

Pool Manager v0.6.0 promotes predictive heating to a common engine for the whole pool season.

## Heating profiles

- `Début de saison • Smart`: heats deliberately to the heat-pump setpoint, then automatically switches the Home Assistant selector to `Automatique`.
- `Automatique`: predictive comfort profile. It prepares likely bathing days while allowing a configurable thermal reserve during long poor-weather periods.
- `Fin de saison • Smart`: uses the same predictive engine with a larger allowed temperature drift to save energy between credible bathing opportunities.
- `Turbo`: remains an explicit immediate override.
- `Désactivé`, `Hors Gel` and forced stop remain higher-priority safety/policy states.

The legacy selector wording `Première chauffe • Smart` is still recognized as a migration alias.

## Forecast reasoning

The engine requests the Home Assistant weather entity's daily forecast and uses up to ten available days. Each day receives a bathing-opportunity score from:

- forecast maximum temperature;
- weather condition and cloud coverage;
- precipitation probability and amount;
- wind speed.

The selected bathing day is exposed as `🏊` in dashboard diagnostics. A good day followed by at least two poor days is treated as a likely last opportunity and receives additional heating margin.

## Heating planning

By default:

- likely bathing time: 16:00;
- water ready target: 11:00;
- preferred main preparation: previous day 12:00–20:00;
- bathing-day top-up: from 07:00 to the ready-by deadline.

The planner calculates the temperature deficit, estimates the PAC °C/h performance and allocates the required hours. A small recovery is deliberately placed on the previous day rather than waiting until the bathing morning. Larger recoveries can span several windows. If preferred windows become insufficient, the engine enters an immediate catch-up path instead of silently missing the comfort target.

A warm/sunny day receives a better heating-quality score. When the day immediately before the bathing opportunity is substantially worse than a recent earlier day, the planner may move a limited part of the preparation (maximum 25%, capped at two hours) to the better day. Most heating remains close to the bathing day to limit thermal losses.

## Long poor-weather periods

When no credible bathing opportunity is visible, the engine does not maintain full setpoint continuously.

Default reserve profiles:

- `Automatique`: setpoint − 2 °C;
- `Fin de saison`: setpoint − 4 °C.

The reserve is recharged only when necessary and in the preferred daytime window. A new bathing opportunity causes an immediate re-plan.

## PAC performance learning

The configured heating rate is the initial model. During real heating with confirmed pump circulation, Pool Manager observes water-temperature gain and stores an exponentially weighted rate in broad outdoor-temperature buckets. Future schedules blend the learned value with the weather-adjusted configured rate. Implausible samples are rejected.

Learning is in-memory in v0.6.0; after an AppDaemon restart the configured baseline is used again until new samples are learned.

## Home Assistant diagnostic sensor

When `entity_chauffage_predictif_status` is configured, AppDaemon publishes a virtual sensor with attributes intended for Mushroom/dashboard use:

- current heating decision;
- water and target temperatures;
- dynamic reserve floor;
- next bathing date and score;
- `last_chance` flag;
- ready-by time;
- active and next heating windows;
- full heating schedule;
- 7–10 day forecast rows with `swim` and `heating` markers;
- estimated and learned PAC heating rates.

See `examples/mushroom_predictive_heating.yaml` for a ready-to-adapt card.

## Migration from v0.5

The v0.5 `fin_saison_*` settings remain accepted as aliases. In particular, an existing `fin_saison_predictif: true` enables the common v0.6 predictive engine. For a clean v0.6 configuration, prefer the new `chauffage_predictif_*` names from `examples/filtration_piscine.yaml`.

Update the Home Assistant selector option `Première chauffe • Smart` to `Début de saison • Smart` when convenient; both strings are understood by the code during migration.
