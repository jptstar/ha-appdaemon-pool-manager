# v0.6.1 — 15-day strategic weather outlook

v0.6.1 separates **weather visibility** from **heating commitment**.

- Up to 15 daily forecasts are retained when Home Assistant provides them.
- J0-J3: strong confidence and operational planning.
- J4-J7: medium-confidence strategic outlook.
- J8-J10: trend-level outlook.
- J11-J15: indicative only.

The distance weighting is applied to the bathing opportunity score, while the raw score remains available for diagnostics. By default, full setpoint recovery is not scheduled until an opportunity enters the 3-day operational horizon. A distant attractive forecast can therefore be displayed as `🏊` without causing immediate multi-day heating. Reserve-floor protection remains active.

Configuration:

```yaml
chauffage_predictif_horizon_jours: 15
chauffage_predictif_horizon_operationnel_jours: 3
```

The predictive status sensor also exposes `next_swim_strategic_score`, `next_swim_confidence`, `forecast_horizon_days`, `operational_horizon_days`, and per-day `strategic_score`, `horizon_weight`, `confidence`, `operational` attributes.
